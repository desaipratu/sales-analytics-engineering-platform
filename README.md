# Sales Analytics Engineering Platform

Modern analytics engineering platform designed to transform raw business data into trusted reporting and analytics.

---

## Project Objective

Build a complete analytics engineering workflow demonstrating ingestion, transformation, warehouse modeling, and reporting.

---

## Business Scenario

Business data exists across multiple systems and reporting is inconsistent.

This platform demonstrates a structured ELT workflow.

---

## Planned Features

- Data Ingestion
- ELT Pipeline
- Warehouse Modeling
- Business Metrics
- Dashboard Reporting

---

## Planned Tech Stack

| Layer | Technology |
|--------|-----------|
| Orchestration | Airflow |
| Transformation | dbt |
